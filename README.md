# awesome-ai-tools
A curated list of Awesome AI Tools

## AI Video Generation
*AI-powered tools for generating videos from text and images.*
- [Reelive](https://reelive.ai) - Unified AI video platform aggregating Sora, Veo, Kling, Hailuo and more models in one place.
- [Sora](https://sora.com) - OpenAI's groundbreaking text-to-video model with unmatched realism and native audio generation.
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com) - Professional-grade AI video suite with advanced motion tracking, inpainting, and precise camera controls.
- [Kling AI](https://klingai.com) - Kuaishou's video generator excelling at long-form clips up to 2 minutes with motion control.
- [Pika](https://pika.art) - Fast and accessible AI video tool with creative stylization and quick generation times.
- [Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai) - Cinematic video generator known for lifelike motion and realistic physics simulation.
- [Google Veo](https://deepmind.google/technologies/veo) - DeepMind's video model featuring native audio synthesis including dialogue and sound effects.
- [Hailuo AI](https://hailuoai.video) - Versatile video platform by MiniMax with text-to-speech, image generation, and voice cloning.
- [Vidu](https://vidu.io) - Chinese AI video tool supporting 4K output with multi-subject consistency from up to 7 reference images.

## AI Video Editing
*AI tools for enhancing and editing existing videos.*
- [Descript](https://descript.com) - Edit video by editing text transcript with AI-powered overdub and filler word removal.
- [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com) - Online video editor with AI features for subtitles, resizing, and content repurposing.
- [Topaz Video AI](https://topazlabs.com/topaz-video-ai) - AI upscaling and enhancement for video resolution, frame rate, and stabilization.
- [HeyGen](https://heygen.com) - AI avatar video generator for creating talking head videos with lip-sync.
- [Synthesia](https://synthesia.io) - Enterprise AI video platform for creating videos with AI avatars and voiceovers.
